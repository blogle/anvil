Living design record · v2

A factory that can show its work.

Anvil is a proposed unattended Kubernetes software factory. An external supervised Ralph loop keeps workers moving; Anvil reconciles evidence and remote-world state until an integrated change is verified, or a human is asked to decide.

Direction, not implementationRuntime and DB undecided

Read this first

This is architecture discussion, not authorization to build a daemon, choose a runtime, or merge changes. The dossier is deliberately opinionated about vocabulary and boundaries, and deliberately undecided about machinery.

Snapshot: 18 Oct 2026 · branch v2

The thesis

Make progress a claim that must be backed by revision-bound evidence. Keep execution in OpenCode and agent-sandbox; keep orchestration in a controller that observes, decides, acts, and can explain why.

Boundary. Anvil is not a local OpenCode replacement. It owns the Commission decision authority and operational protocol around workers, not the worker’s conversation or model.

Decision status

Agreed direction

Evidence first

World state is authoritative. Claims from sessions, todos, and reports are useful signals, never proof.

Provisional

Selective history

Persist durable artifacts and a journal of decisions; do not default to full event sourcing or transcript cloning.

Open

Corrective policy

The exact thresholds and strategy-selection policy for Wait, Repair, Refresh, and friends remain to be designed.

Three useful cuts through the design

01 / vocabulary

Name the thing

Campaigns contain Commissions. Assignments delegate responsibility. Attempts are bounded strategies. Candidates identify possible revisions.

02 / control loop

Reconcile, don’t choreograph

Signals trigger reconciliation; the observed world is read again before a typed Decision selects the next Operation.

03 / uncertainty

Keep the blanks visible

Open decisions have owners, tests, and exit criteria. “TBD” is not a design boundary.

Non-goals

  • Choosing Rust, Go, TypeScript, Temporal, DBOS, or another runtime.
  • Implementing the v2 daemon, an authorization model, or a merge policy by implication.
  • Calling an LLM transcript a source of truth.
  • Promising a general semantic correctness oracle or automatic acceptance of UI behavior.
